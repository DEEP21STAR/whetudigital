Common questions

Do I own the code?
Yes — 100%. Everything we build is yours. We deliver source files (HTML/CSS/JS) and you can host them anywhere. No lock-in, no ongoing fees unless you choose a retainer.
What's your tech stack?
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no React, no WordPress, no Webflow. Fast, portable, and maintainable. We use OKLCH colour systems, GSAP animations, and modern CSS. Everything runs in a browser with zero build chain.
How long does a project take?
Starter: 5–7 days. Professional: 2–3 weeks. Enterprise: project-specific — we'll scope it in the brief call. We work fast because we don't use frameworks that need build steps.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Standard split: 50% deposit to start, 50% on delivery. For Enterprise projects we can discuss milestone-based billing. All invoices are in NZD with GST.
Can you host and maintain my site?
We set you up on free hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages). For ongoing maintenance, content updates, and feature additions, we offer monthly retainers from $150/mo. Ask us during onboarding.
