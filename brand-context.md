## Target Clients

### Clarity
- Age: 25–45
- Situation: Earning OK, but no visibility on spending. Probably using YNAB or a spreadsheet halfheartedly.
- Pain: "I know I should be saving more but I don't know where it goes."
- NZ angle: Cost of living pressure. Rent eating income. Want to feel in control.

### Property Tracker NZ
- Age: 40–65
- Situation: 1–4 properties. Using spreadsheets. Come tax time it's a mess.
- Pain: "I dread sending my accountant the numbers every year."
- NZ angle: IRD compliance, mileage claims, GST — specific NZ rules not covered by overseas tools.

### Whetū Digital Agency Services
- Small NZ businesses wanting a professional digital presence without $10k agency quotes
- Early-stage founders who need a product built, not a 20-page proposal

## Pricing Philosophy

- **$19/month** is the anchor price across products — accessible, not cheap
- Annual plan ($149/year) = 2 months free — strong incentive to commit
- No free tier that undercuts the paid plan — free trial (14 days), then pay
- "Less than a coffee a week" framing works for NZ market
- Commercial rights: all Whetū-delivered products are commercially licensed
